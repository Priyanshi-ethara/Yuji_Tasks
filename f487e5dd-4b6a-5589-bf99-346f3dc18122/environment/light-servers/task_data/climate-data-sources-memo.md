# Memo — which temperature goes into the string Voc check?
From: M. Penrose, Head of Engineering · To: Design desk · Date: 24 September 2026

The Site climate list carries three temperatures per station:
1. Extreme min design (°C) — the extreme annual mean minimum dry-bulb, 1991–2020. This is T_min under Edition 4.
2. Mean min coldest month (°C) — what Edition 3 used. Too warm: it is an average of nightly minima.
3. Record low (°C) — a single historical reading. Too cold for design and not what the standard asks for.

Other sources are not used: weather apps show today's temperature; climate summaries give annual averages; old exports of the list miss later revisions (Tallow Vale was re-analysed in September 2026 after the W31 frost trips).
