# W44 design review — summary (closed 30 October 2026)
14 designs: Approved 10, Redesign – overvoltage 2, Redesign – below MPPT 2, Redesign – overcurrent 0.
Note: one designer submitted a register line with the module register's Kestrel coefficient (−0.138) treated as %/°C; the desk recomputed with the datasheet's −138 mV/°C and returned the design for overvoltage.
Note: AR-6.0 limit is 550 V (catalogue, notice ARN-2026-08), not the 600 V on the old quick-reference sheet.
