Coefficient units: the Kestrel KP-470 datasheet gives its Voc coefficient as -138 mV/°C, which DS-6 clause 4.2 treats as a per-module change: Voc(T) = 50.90 V + (-138/1000) × (T − 25), roughly -0.271 %/°C. I did not read it as -0.138 %/°C, which is what the module register's percentage column shows; that reading understates the cold Voc and would have passed the Gideon Varcoe design wrongly. Every other datasheet states %/°C.

Inverter limits and the units they cover (all from the Notion inverter catalogue pages): Volta 8K: 600 V max DC input, MPPT lower bound 200 V, 20 A per tracker; Volta 8K HV: 1000 V max DC input, MPPT lower bound 250 V, 20 A per tracker; Volta 5K: 600 V max DC input, MPPT lower bound 120 V, 20 A per tracker; Ardent AR-6.0: 550 V max DC input, MPPT lower bound 100 V, 17.5 A per tracker; Brightline BL-10: 1000 V max DC input, MPPT lower bound 300 V, 30 A per tracker. The Ardent AR-6.0 limit is 550 V (Notion catalogue, manufacturer notice ARN-2026-08), not the 600 V printed on the June quick-reference sheet and the Box spec sheet.

Design minimum temperatures: T_min is the 'Extreme min design (°C)' value of each site's climate station in the Airtable Site climate list (DS-6 clause 2.3), not the coldest-month mean minimum, not the record low, not today's weather from the weather services and not the May export of the list (which still shows Tallow Vale at -19.8 °C instead of -21.2 °C).
