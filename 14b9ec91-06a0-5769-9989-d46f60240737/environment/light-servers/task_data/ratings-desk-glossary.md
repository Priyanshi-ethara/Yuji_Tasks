# Ratings desk glossary

- **Rating day** - the UTC day the day-ahead rating applies to (00:00-24:00).
- **Station inputs** - a station's highest rating-day temperature and lowest rating-day wind.
- **Ambient band / wind band** - Table 2 bands, labels T1-T6 and W1-W4.
- **Line rating** - the Table 3 value for the circuit's conductor and bands.
- **Series element** - a cable section (or other equipment) carrying the full circuit current in series with the line.
- **Limiting element** - 'Overhead line' or 'Cable section', whichever sets the circuit rating.
- **Constrained** - forecast peak load at or above 90 % of the issued rating.
- **Reissue / amendment** - MetDesk replacing a station's forecast in the service between or after routine runs.
- **Knot (kn)** - 1 nautical mile per hour = 0.5144 m/s.
