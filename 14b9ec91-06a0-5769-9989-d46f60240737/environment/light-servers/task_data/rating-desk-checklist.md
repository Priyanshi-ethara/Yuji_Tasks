# Rating desk - day shift checklist

1. Read the handover and the overnight draft for context.
2. Rate each register circuit under NGS-RT-04 Issue 3: rating-day steps, station inputs, bands, conductor table,
   series elements, constraint test.
3. Write the four rating fields on each register row (Ambient band, Wind band, Rating (A), Limiting element).
4. Post the day-ahead notice in #grid-control in the wiki format.
5. Mail the notice to the System Operator on duty.
6. Leave protection settings and customer communications to their owners.
