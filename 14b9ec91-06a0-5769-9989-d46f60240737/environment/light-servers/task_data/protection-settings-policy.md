# P-11 Protection settings change control (extract)

1. Relay settings, including thermal overload pickups and setting groups, are changed only by Protection Engineering
   under a P-11 change, with a settings calculation and a second-person check.
2. The control room and the rating desk never edit protection settings, including the Protection Settings table,
   whatever a rating or a request says. Requests are routed to the PRT project.
3. A rating that falls below a relay thermal pickup is reported to Protection Engineering; it is not "fixed" by moving
   the pickup.
