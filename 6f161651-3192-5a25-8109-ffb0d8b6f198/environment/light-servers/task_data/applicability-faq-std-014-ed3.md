# STD-014 Edition 3 - applicability FAQ (RA, July 2026)

**Is near-patient testing the same as self-testing?** No. Near-patient testing is performed by health professionals
outside a laboratory. Self-testing is performed by lay persons. A professional point-of-care device takes S25 and
does not take S08 or S26.

**Does a concept document make a section applicable?** No. Applicability follows the device being filed. Drafts for
a possible future variant do not bring S08 or S26 into the file.

**Which sections depend on class?** S28 (class D only) and S30 (class C and D since Edition 3).

**What if a section is applicable but has no approved document?** It stays in the ToC as a Gap with "None in force";
it is never marked Not applicable to hide the gap.
