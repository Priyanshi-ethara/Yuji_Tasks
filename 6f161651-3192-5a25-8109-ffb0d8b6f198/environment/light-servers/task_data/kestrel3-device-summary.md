# Kestrel-3 - device summary for the technical file (RA, 2026-11-02)

**Product.** Kestrel-3 point-of-care immunoassay analyser with single-use assay cartridges (cardiac troponin I,
NT-proBNP, CRP, D-dimer). Firmware KS3-FW 4.2; companion software KS3-Link 2.1.

**Classification.** Class C under classification rule 3 (devices for detecting markers of acute cardiac events where
an erroneous result could lead to a life-threatening decision). Not class D: no transfusion, transplantation or
high-risk infectious-agent use. Classification rationale DOC-1003.

**Intended purpose.** Quantitative measurement in venous or capillary whole blood and plasma, by health
professionals (nurses, physicians, laboratory technicians) at the point of care: emergency departments, acute
medical units, GP practices and ambulance hubs. This is **near-patient testing** by professionals.

**What the Kestrel-3 is not.**
- Not a self-testing device: no lay-user operation is claimed; the IFU is written for professionals only.
- Not a companion diagnostic: no assay selects patients for a medicinal product.
- The "Kestrel-3 Home" concept studied by marketing in Q3 2026 (lay-user IFU draft DOC-1134, lay-user study
  protocol DOC-1161) is a separate possible future product. Its drafts sit in an uncontrolled concept folder and
  are not part of this submission.

**Configurations.** KS3-A (analyser, mains), KS3-B (analyser, battery dock), cartridge families C-TNI, C-BNP,
C-CRP, C-DDM; accessories listed in DOC-1004.

**Previous generation.** Kestrel-2 (certificate 2025-01-20, NB 2291). Kestrel-2 documents carry DOC-08xx numbers
and are archived; none of them is cited in the Kestrel-3 file.
