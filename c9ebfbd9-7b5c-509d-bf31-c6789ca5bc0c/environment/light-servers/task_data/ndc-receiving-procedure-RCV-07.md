# RCV-07 Receiving refrigerated shipments at the NDC

Revision 4, Warehouse Operations, effective 1 July 2026.

1. The NDC receipt scan (gate scanner or handheld) transfers custody from the lane to the NDC. Until the scan, the
   shipment is in lane custody whatever the carrier's systems say.
2. If the gate scanner is down, pallets wait on the dock in the receiving bay until a handheld is brought; the receiving
   supervisor logs the delay. Dock dwell is lane custody time.
3. After the scan the shipment logger is stopped within 90 minutes and returned with the lot paperwork. Readings after the
   receipt scan belong to NDC storage and are assessed under the NDC stock procedures, not the lane.
4. Cold Room alarms are handled under WH-ALM-02 (PagerDuty service "NDC cold rooms").
