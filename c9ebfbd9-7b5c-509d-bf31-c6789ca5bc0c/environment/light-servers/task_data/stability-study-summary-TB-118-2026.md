# Stability study summary - TB-118 Pellumab 150 mg/mL (STS-118-2026-03)

Stability Science, M. Solberg. Approved 9 June 2026; basis of specification Edition F.

## Accelerated arm
Arrhenius fit of the main degradant (high-molecular-weight species) at 5, 15, 25 and 30 degC, 6 months:
- ln k versus 1/T linear, r^2 = 0.991.
- Activation energy Ea = 112.0 kJ/mol (95 % CI 104.1-119.6).
- Edition E used the interim value 104.5 kJ/mol from the 3-month pull; superseded by Edition F.

## Consequences for distribution
With Ea = 112.0 kJ/mol, MKT weights warm readings more strongly than the 83.144 kJ/mol default: a shipment that spends
hours above 8 degC can show an MKT above the 8.0 degC limit although its default-dH MKT is below it.
TOR budget unchanged at 120 h.
