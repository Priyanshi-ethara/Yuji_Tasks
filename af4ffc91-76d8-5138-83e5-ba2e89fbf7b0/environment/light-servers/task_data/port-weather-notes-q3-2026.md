Climate notes used for planning
- Baltic coal ports (Ventspils, Riga, Gdansk): summer showers are short and frequent; terminals keep working in light
  rain for coal but close hatches in heavy rain to protect the fines.
- Rouen and Ghent grain: any rain closes the hatches on grain; dust hoods are lifted when hatches reopen.
- Constanta: August thunderstorms are short and heavy; harvest-peak stockpile shortfalls are a separate, recurring issue.
- Antwerp and Rotterdam urea: urea is hygroscopic; hatches close at the first steady rain.
- Narvik and Lulea pellets: pellets are worked in light rain; squalls and thunderstorms close the hatches for safety.
