# Laytime glossary — Merrow claims desk

Arrived ship: a vessel at the port (whether in berth or not, where the charter says WIBON) that may tender notice of readiness.
NOR: notice of readiness. Under our 2026 charters it can be tendered at any time of day.
Turn time: running hours after NOR before laytime starts, unless cargo work starts sooner.
Laytime allowed: bill of lading quantity divided by the daily rate, in days of 24 hours (we work in minutes).
Weather working day: see each charter's clause 17; the charters define it in their own words and those words govern.
SHEX EIU / SHINC: Sundays (and in some charters holidays) excepted even if used / Sundays and holidays included. The
excepted hours are set by each charter (Holmfirth: Saturday 12:00 to Monday 08:00 and local holidays; Kestrel: Saturday
17:00 to Monday 08:00, holidays count).
Shifting: anchor aweigh / letting go to all fast at the next berth; excluded from laytime by clause 18.
Time used: laytime counted plus time on demurrage. Time saved: laytime allowed less laytime counted, when positive.
Demurrage: per day pro rata (rate x minutes / 1440). Despatch: half the demurrage rate on laytime saved.
Once on demurrage: after the laytime allowed is used up, time runs continuously to completion; the laytime exceptions
(weather, weekends, holidays, shifting) do not apply to time on demurrage under any of our charters.
Statement of facts (SOF): the agents' log of the call, signed by master and agents; the record of times.
Hatch closures: the SOF's footer list of the periods the hatches were shut for weather.
