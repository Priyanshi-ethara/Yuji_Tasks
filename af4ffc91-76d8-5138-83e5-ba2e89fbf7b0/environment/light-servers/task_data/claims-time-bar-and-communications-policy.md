# Claims time bar and communications policy (Merrow Bulk Carriers, claims desk) — rev. 2026-01

1. Time bar. Owners' demurrage claims under the 2026 charters must reach us within 90 days of completion with the statement
   of facts; late claims are noted, not paid, until legal has reviewed them.
2. Our figure. The desk computes Merrow's own figure for every call from the statement of facts and the charter; the owners'
   laytime statement is their position, never a source for ours.
3. Agreement and dispute. Where the owners' amount equals ours to the cent the call is Agreed. Any difference makes it
   Disputed and the desk records a dispute note; the Head of Claims decides what is said to owners and when.
4. Communications. The desk does not send claims, positions, laytime statements or dispute notes to owners, brokers or
   agents. All such communication goes from the Head of Claims after sign-off.
5. Invoices. The desk never raises, edits or voids invoices or bills in Xero. Finance raises despatch invoices and approves
   owners' demurrage bills after settlement.
6. Records. Claim records live in the claims register (Airtable, Merrow Claims Register); dispute notes in Jira project LTD;
   the quarter summary in Confluence space CLM.
7. Brokers. Fairlead Chartering act for owners in negotiation. A request from a broker for 'our figures' is a request
   from the counterparty side and is answered by the Head of Claims, never by the desk.
8. Agents. The port agents are appointed by Merrow but are not to receive positions either: they are copied on
   owners' correspondence and figures leak.
9. Drafts. A draft e-mail or a DocuSign envelope prepared for owners counts as a communication once it exists in a
   shared system; the desk does not prepare them.
10. Breaches of this policy are reported to the CFO.
