# MBC/HOL Grainvoy 2026 — laytime, demurrage and despatch (clauses 14 to 24)

Voyage charter form for milling wheat from French, Belgian and Black Sea ports, 2026 programme, between Holmfirth Bulk Ltd, Hull (Owners) and Merrow Bulk Carriers Ltd (Charterers). Signed 2 March 2026.

Owners: Holmfirth Bulk Ltd. Charterers: Merrow Bulk Carriers Ltd. Cargo: milling wheat. Currency of account: USD.

### Clause 14 — Notice of readiness

Notice of readiness may be tendered at any time, day or night, by e-mail to the shippers or receivers and to charterers' agents, once the vessel has arrived at the port and is in all respects ready, whether in berth or not (WIBON), whether in port or not, whether customs cleared or not.

### Clause 15 — Commencement of laytime

Laytime shall commence 6 running hours after notice of readiness has been tendered (turn time), unless loading sooner commences, in which case time shall count from the commencement of loading. Turn time runs whatever the day of the week.

### Clause 16 — Rate

The cargo shall be loaded at the average rate of 8,000 metric tonnes per weather working day of 24 consecutive hours, Sundays and holidays excepted, even if used (SHEX EIU): the period from 12:00 on Saturday to 08:00 on Monday, and any local public holiday at the port from 00:00 to 24:00, shall not count as laytime whether or not the vessel works. The rate applies to the bill of lading quantity.

### Clause 17 — Weather working day

'Weather working day' means a day of 24 consecutive hours on which time counts unless, and only for so long as, weather actually prevents the loading of the vessel.

### Clause 18 — Shifting

Time used in shifting from the anchorage or waiting place to the first berth, and between berths, shall not count as laytime, from anchor aweigh or letting go until all fast at the next berth. Shifting expenses for Owners' account from anchorage to first berth; between berths for Charterers' account.

### Clause 19 — Completion

Laytime shall end on completion of loading. Time for the issue of documents, any draft survey after completion, unmooring and sailing formalities shall not count.

### Clause 20 — Demurrage

Charterers shall pay demurrage at the rate of USD 18,500 per day or pro rata for any part of a day for all time by which the laytime allowed is exceeded.

### Clause 21 — Despatch

Owners shall pay despatch money at half the demurrage rate, USD 9,250 per day pro rata, on laytime saved.

### Clause 22 — Stevedores and shore installations

Stevedores at the loading port shall be appointed and paid by Charterers or their shippers/receivers and shall work under the Master's supervision. The shore installations (conveyors, shiploaders, grabs, stockpiles and hoppers) are provided by the terminal at Charterers' arrangement.

### Clause 23 — Statement of facts and claims

The agents' statement of facts, signed by the Master and the agents, shall be the record of the times of the call. Claims for demurrage shall be presented within 90 days of completion of discharge with the statement of facts and a laytime statement, failing which the claim shall be deemed waived.

### Clause 24 — Law and arbitration

English law; London arbitration, LMAA Terms current at the date of the reference; small claims procedure below USD 100,000 or equivalent.
