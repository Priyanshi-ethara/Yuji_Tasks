# Port notes — Rotterdam, Netherlands (claims desk reference, rev. June 2026)

Terminal: Botlek bulk berth 7; Maas anchorages 3-5 for waiting tonnage.

Public holidays relevant to Q3 2026: None in September.

Working pattern: terminals work 24 hours, seven days, unless the berth schedule says otherwise; weekend work does not change how a charter's excepted periods are treated.
Pilotage and towage: compulsory; the time from anchor aweigh to all fast is logged by the agents.
Agents: charterers' nominated agents log statements of facts from the vessel's arrival to sailing and file them in Box within 10 days.

## Local practice notes
- Draft surveys: initial and final surveys by an independent surveyor; an intermediate survey may be requested by shippers.
- Meal breaks: gangs stop for 30-60 minutes around midday and midnight in most ports; time counts.
- Rain: open-hatch cargoes (grain, urea, coal fines) are protected by closing hatches; ore pellets are normally worked in light rain.
- Equipment: shiploader and conveyor faults are logged by the terminal's control room and reported to the agents.
- Night work: gangs for night shifts are ordered by the agents on the day; the pool confirms by 16:00.
- Documents: bills of lading are signed by the agents on the master's authority once the final figure is agreed.
- Waiting: when the berth is occupied the vessel waits at the anchorage named in the agents' line-up; NOR is tendered on arrival there.
