# Self-service team - November briefing (Carys Llewellyn)

What we work: the Residential enquiries view. Business enquiries go to the business desk; faults and owed actions go to the
owning teams.

Staffing rule of thumb: one person per five enquiries arriving from a Desk Quality batch on top of the normal flow.

November so far
- Week 45 batch: 4 enquiries reached us.
- Week 46 batch (2026-11-18): Tobias's draft says 10 enquiries - two people on Thursday morning.

Answer standards: plain English (Welsh on request), link to the help page, no promises on dates the owning team hasn't
confirmed, close the loop on the ticket with an internal note before handing back.

What does not reach us
- Faults (incidents) and owed actions (tasks) go to the owning teams' queues, whatever the customer asked first.
- Business enquiries are answered by the business desk.
- Anything on hold with Billing Operations or Field Operations stays with them until they hand it back.

Rota (week 47)
- Monday to Wednesday: two people, 08:00-16:00. Thursday: one person plus whoever the Desk Quality batch needs.
- Welsh-language enquiries: Eleri (Mon, Thu), Rhodri (Tue, Wed, Fri).

Top enquiry themes in November
- The April price change letter (what it means for the bill).
- Contract end dates and what happens after the minimum term.
- Adding Wi-Fi Max pods; TV channel line-ups; upgrade options from copper to fibre.

Handing back
- If an enquiry turns out to be a fault or needs an action from another team, hand it back to the front line with an
  internal note saying why; do not retype it yourselves.
