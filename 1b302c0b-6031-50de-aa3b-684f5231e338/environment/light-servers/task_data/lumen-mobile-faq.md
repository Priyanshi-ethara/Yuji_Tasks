# Lumen Mobile - desk FAQ

- SIM-only and handset plans share the network (prod_mobile_sim). The handset itself is prod_mobile_handset.
- No network at all after a SIM swap is a provisioning issue (network service), not the handset.
- Handset faults (screen, battery, buttons) are warranty repairs for 24 months.
- Weekend SIM swaps are provisioned on the next working day if the provisioning queue backs up.
- SIM swap: the old SIM stops when the new one is provisioned; until then the customer keeps service on the old SIM if
  they still have it. A swap stuck in the provisioning queue leaves the new SIM with no network (MC-401, 14-17 Nov).
- eSIM: issued by QR code from the Lumen app; a failed eSIM download is a provisioning matter (prod_mobile_sim).
- Porting a mobile number: PAC or STAC by text to 65075, valid 30 days; the port completes next working day.
- Roaming: included in 50 destinations on all plans; roaming not working abroad is a network-service fault.
- Handset warranty repair: the customer is sent a prepaid repair pack; a loan handset is offered to Priority Assistance
  customers. Screen, battery, charging port and button faults are hardware (prod_mobile_handset) as long as calls and
  data work with the SIM in another handset.
- Data allowance questions and bill shock alerts are billing enquiries (prod_account_general).
- Lost or stolen phone: bar the SIM from the account page at once (any agent can bar); the replacement SIM is a SIM swap.
- Signal at home weak but fine elsewhere: coverage enquiry first (coverage checker), a network fault only if the checker
  shows full coverage and other customers nearby report the same.
