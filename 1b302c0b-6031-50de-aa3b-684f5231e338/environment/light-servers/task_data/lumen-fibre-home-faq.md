# Fibre Home - desk FAQ

- Plans: Fibre Home 150 / 500 / 900; guaranteed minimum speeds 120 / 400 / 700 Mbps measured at the router by line test.
- Wi-Fi Max is a separate add-on (prod_wifi_max): mesh pods added from the Lumen app (Wi-Fi Max > Add pod); no engineer needed.
- Speeds below the minimum at the router: line or network cause. At plan speed at the router but slow on devices: in-home Wi-Fi
  (2.4 GHz congestion, distance, walls).
- The fibre wall socket (ONT socket) is part of the access installation; a damaged socket is a repair visit.
- Digital home phone runs through the router; in a power cut it stops unless the customer has the battery pack (free for PA).

Line tests and outages
- Run the line test from the customer's account before booking anything. 'Line at plan speed' means the shortfall is
  inside the home; 'CRC errors' or 'below minimum at the router' means the line or network.
- Check the NOC incident digest for the customer's exchange or cabinet first. Customers inside an open incident are linked
  to the NOC parent and told the restoration estimate the NOC publishes.

Moving home
- The customer books the move; Field Operations books the install at the new address. Where Lumen moves the install date
  (capacity, access), the customer is texted the new date and the ticket keeps tracking the install.
- The old address stays live until the day before the new install, so there is no gap for the customer.

Router and Wi-Fi
- Router lights: white = online, amber = no fibre signal, red = authentication failure, flashing white = updating.
- Wi-Fi Max pods: up to three per home; each pod must sit within two rooms of the router or another pod.
- Customers on Fibre Home 900 need a Wi-Fi 6 device to see more than about 500 Mbps over Wi-Fi.
