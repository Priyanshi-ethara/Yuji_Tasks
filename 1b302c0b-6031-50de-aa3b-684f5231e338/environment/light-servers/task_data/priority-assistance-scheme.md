# Priority Assistance scheme - desk summary (2026)

Purpose: customers who depend on their Lumen service for their safety or health get faster handling.

Register
- Held in Salesforce on the contact: Priority Assistance flag, category (telecare alarm dependency, home medical equipment,
  sight loss - accessible contact, other), date registered. The register is maintained by the Customer Care team.
- A business account holder can be on the register where they live at the premises or the scheme requires it
  (for example a sole trader living above the workshop, or a parish warden scheme line).
- Agents never disclose register membership to anyone but the customer.

What the customer is owed
- A callback within four hours of any contact, by phone unless the contact record asks otherwise (large print, Welsh).
- At least High priority on every ticket (categorisation guide 5.3).
- Free engineer visits for faults that affect a telecare device.

Categories in use (Q4 2026): telecare alarm dependency 61 %, home medical equipment 18 %, sight loss 9 %, hearing loss 7 %, other 5 %.

Checking the register (desk)
- The register lives on the Salesforce contact, not on the ticket and not on the organisation name. Open the contact the
  ticket's requester maps to and read the Priority Assistance flag and category; the date registered shows how long the
  customer has been on it, not whether the floor applies (it applies from the day the flag is set).
- A customer who says on a call that they rely on a pendant alarm or oxygen concentrator but has no flag is referred to
  Customer Care for registration (Customer Care referral form). Until the flag is set the ticket follows the plain matrix.
- Carers and relatives can ask on the customer's behalf; the ticket is still the registered customer's ticket.
- Register entries are re-confirmed with the customer every twelve months by Customer Care. A lapsed confirmation does not
  remove the flag; only Customer Care removes it, and only at the customer's request or on a closed account.

Service notes for agents
- Telecare and digital voice: many pendant alarms dial out over the phone line. Since the move to digital voice they need
  the router powered; the free battery pack covers a power cut for at least one hour. Telecare compatibility questions
  are answered from the telecare compatibility list on the help site; untested alarm models are checked by Field Operations.
- Faults on a PA line that stop a telecare device working are attended free of charge, including the socket and router.
- PA callbacks are logged on the PA callback board by the owning team; the desk does not ring the customer itself.
- Welsh-speaking PA customers are called back by the Welsh rota where the contact record asks for Welsh.
