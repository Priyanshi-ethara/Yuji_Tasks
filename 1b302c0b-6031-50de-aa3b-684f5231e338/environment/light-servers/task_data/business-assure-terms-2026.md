# Business Assure - service terms extract (2026)

Business Assure is the enhanced care contract for business accounts.

- Fault response: incidents are handled one priority step above the standard matrix (normal to high; high to urgent).
- Whole-site loss of service is handled as urgent with a one-hour first response.
- Enquiries, billing questions and owed actions (installs, moves, ports) are handled at the standard priority.
- The contract is recorded on the Salesforce account (Business Assure flag) and mirrored as the ba_contract organisation tag
  in Lumen Desk.
- Customers without Business Assure are handled at the standard matrix whatever their size or spend.

Accounts on Business Assure in the Westerby and Llanfair Mor patches (Q4 2026): 31, including Calder Freight Ltd,
Harbourside Veterinary Group and Westerby Parish Council.

What Business Assure covers
- All Business Fibre, Business Voice and Business Mobile services on the contracted account, at every site listed on the
  account record. Services at a director's home address are not covered unless listed.
- Named technical contacts (up to four) can raise faults on behalf of the account; their tickets carry the same uplift.
- Planned maintenance is notified seven days ahead by email to the named contacts; planned work is not a fault.

What it does not change
- The type of a ticket: Business Assure never turns an enquiry into an incident or an owed action into a fault.
- Billing disputes, which follow the standard billing process and timescales.
- Priority Assistance, which is a separate register on the contact and can apply to a business account holder as well.

Service credits
- Credits for missed Business Assure fault response targets are calculated monthly by Billing Operations from the
  priority and timestamps held on the ticket, and applied to the next invoice automatically. The desk does not promise
  or quote credits to customers.
