# Customer communications policy - service desk (2026)

1. Only the team that owns a ticket writes to the customer. Back-office teams (Desk Quality, Desk Tooling, Billing
   Operations) add internal notes only.
2. A public comment on a ticket is emailed to the requester at once. There is no recall.
3. Notes added through a ticket update are public unless marked internal.
4. Priority Assistance callbacks are made by the owning team from the PA callback line.
5. Never confirm a fix, a date or a refund to a customer unless the system of record shows it.
6. Outage communications go by NOC SMS, not ticket comments.
7. Identity: before discussing an account, verify the caller (account number plus two of: postcode, date of birth,
   last payment amount). Carers on the account record can be spoken to for that account.
8. Welsh: customers who ask for Welsh are answered in Welsh by the Welsh rota; the lang_cy tag records the request.
9. Accessible formats: large print, braille and audio letters on request, recorded on the Salesforce contact.
10. Tone: plain English, no internal codes, no blame on other teams or suppliers, no guesses about restoration times.
11. Records: every customer contact by the owning team is recorded on the ticket (a public comment if it went to the
    customer, an internal note if it did not). SMS and post sent outside Lumen Desk are noted internally.
12. Complaints: a customer who says they want to complain is told the complaint reference and the eight-week deadline
    (see complaints-and-feedback-handling).
