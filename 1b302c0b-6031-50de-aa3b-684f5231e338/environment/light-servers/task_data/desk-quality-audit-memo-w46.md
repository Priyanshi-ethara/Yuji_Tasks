# Desk Quality audit - week 46 (memo)

From: Marisol Teague, Desk Quality lead. Date: 16 November 2026.

Sample: 214 open tickets touched by the front line between 9 and 16 November. Flagged: 28 (13 %), up from 12 in week 45,
mostly because of the Westerby exchange outage on 16 November and an IVR change that pushed callers asking questions into
the 'voice fault' option.

Patterns
- IVR option taken as the type: enquiries logged as incidents.
- Pre-June product tags still applied by two old macros (fixed 15 November).
- Priority set by how upset the customer sounded rather than by the matrix; Business Assure uplift missed twice.
- Contact reasons chosen from the first screen of the drop-down rather than by meaning (duplicate charges, moving home).
- One customer contact typed as a problem.

Batch: 2026-11-18, all 28 flagged tickets, one categoriser. Output: the tickets recategorised in Lumen Desk, one log row
each, the queue health note. The audit flags say what looked wrong; they are not the answer.

How the sample was drawn
- Every open ticket updated by the front line in the week (phone, web, email and chat queues), excluding NOC parents and
  tickets already solved. Each was checked against guide edition 4: type, matrix priority with the Business Assure and
  Priority Assistance steps from Salesforce, catalogue product, Annex B contact reason, operational tags intact.
- A ticket is flagged when any of the four categorisation fields is doubtful. The flag text records what caught the
  auditor's eye ('type to check', 'priority to check', 'product to check', 'reason to check'); it is not a verdict.

What the flags covered
- Faults: IVR-led mistypes, total-loss tickets left at normal during WK-04, a Business Assure uplift missed.
- Owed actions: installs and a port logged as faults or enquiries.
- Billing: duplicate charges coded without checking whether the refund had gone through; a fee charged in error.
- Enquiries and feedback: enquiries logged as incidents from the 'voice fault' option; feedback at the wrong priority.

Actions agreed at the Desk Quality meeting of 12 November
- Front line refresher on the impact matrix (Priti Shah to run, w47).
- IVR wording for the 'voice fault' option to be reviewed with Desk Tooling.
- Desk Quality to recategorise the flagged tickets as one batch and publish the queue health note the same day.
