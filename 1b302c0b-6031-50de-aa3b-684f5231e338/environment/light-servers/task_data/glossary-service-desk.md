# Service desk glossary

- Lumen Desk - the hosted help desk where tickets live.
- Owning team - the team a ticket is assigned to; only it solves, closes or writes to the customer.
- Desk Quality - audits categorisation and runs recategorisation batches.
- Desk Tooling - administers Lumen Desk (fields, rules, macros).
- Priority Assistance (PA) - register of customers who depend on their service for safety or health.
- Business Assure (BA) - enhanced business care contract.
- NOC parent - a problem ticket for a shared network cause.
- Self-service worklist - residential enquiries answered by the self-service team.
- Queue health note - Desk Quality's note per batch: batch size, type mix and priority mix.
- Short form - the cr_<code> form of a contact reason (Annex B).
- Matrix priority - the priority the impact table in guide 5.1 gives, after the Business Assure step (5.2) and the
  Priority Assistance floor (5.3).
- Total loss - the product the ticket is about does not work at all (no connection, no dial tone, no picture on any
  channel, no mobile network).
- Degraded service - the product works but below what was sold (slow, intermittent, dropping).
- Owed action - something Lumen has committed to do for the customer by a date: visit, install, port.
- Donor network - the network a number is being ported away from; it must release the number.
- ONT - optical network terminal: the fibre wall socket and box at the customer's premises.
- IVR - the phone menu; the option a caller picks is not the ticket type.
- Macro - a saved Lumen Desk action that applies a set of changes and an internal note in one go.
- Audit flag - a row in the Audit flags table of the Desk Quality base marking a ticket for recategorisation.
- Recategorisation log - the Desk Quality base table with one row per ticket recategorised in a batch.
- Field Operations - schedules and carries out engineer visits and installs.
- Billing Operations - raises refunds, credits and billing corrections.
- WK-04 - the Westerby exchange total-loss incident of 16 November 2026 (NOC parent ticket on Lumen Desk).
