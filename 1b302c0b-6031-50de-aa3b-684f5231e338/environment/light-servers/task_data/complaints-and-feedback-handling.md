# Complaints and feedback - desk handling

- A compliment is feedback: type question, priority low; pass it to the person's manager.
- A complaint about a fault or a bill is categorised as that fault or billing error; the complaint process runs alongside.
- A complaint about service in general (attitude, waiting times) is feedback with contact reason
  cr_feedback_complaint_general and goes to Customer Care.
- Marketing opt-outs are preference changes: question, low; record the opt-out in Salesforce marketing preferences
  (Customer Care does this, not the desk).

Timescales
- Complaints are acknowledged within two working days by Customer Care and resolved within eight weeks; after eight weeks
  (or on deadlock) the customer may go to the communications ombudsman.
- The desk's job is to categorise the underlying contact correctly; Customer Care runs the complaint record itself.

Feedback
- Compliments: type question, priority low, product as guide 4.3 (the product the feedback is about; on a single-product
  account, that product); contact reason cr_feedback_compliment (R02). The owning team forwards the compliment to the
  person's manager.
- Suggestions (a feature the customer would like) are feedback as well; Customer Care collates them monthly.

Preference changes
- Marketing opt-out, paper bills, contact by letter only: question, low, prod_account_general; Customer Care records the
  preference in Salesforce the same day. The desk does not edit Salesforce.
- Accessibility feedback (letters too small, website hard to use) is passed to Customer Care for the accessibility log as
  well as being categorised as feedback.
