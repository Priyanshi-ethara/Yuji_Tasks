# Business Voice - desk FAQ

- Hosted VoIP lines on Business Fibre (prod_biz_voip).
- Call divert: set in the Business Voice portal (Lines > Divert), or by dialling *21* + number + #.
- Calls dropping mid-call: site circuit or router first; platform-wide drops are rare and show on the hosted voice monitor.
- Porting in: the losing (donor) network must release the numbers; our side books the date. Delays at the donor are chased
  by the porting desk.
- Business Assure applies to Business Voice incidents as to any incident on the account.
- Hunt groups: set in the portal (Groups > Hunt); ring order sequential, simultaneous or longest idle. A hunt-group
  add-on is a separate product line on the account but is tagged prod_biz_voip on tickets.
- Voicemail to email: portal (Lines > Voicemail); a missing voicemail email is a configuration question unless the
  platform monitor shows a fault.
- Emergency calls: each line has a registered address for 999 calls; changing it is an account change done by the
  business desk.
- Porting timescales: single-line ports 7 working days, multi-line 10 working days from the donor's acceptance. The
  porting desk tracks every port on the porting tracker; the desk reads the tracker, never edits it.
- Call quality (echo, robotic voice) on one site: router and circuit first; across several customers: hosted voice monitor
  and the NOC digest.
- Portal access is for the account's named admins; a lost admin password is reset by the business desk after verification.
