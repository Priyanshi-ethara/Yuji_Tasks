# Worked example — chilled water to electricity equivalent (from the FY2025 workbook, Albany)

FY2025 Albany delivered 2,812,400 ton-hours.
2,812,400 × 12,000 Btu = 33,748,800,000 Btu; ÷ 3,412.14 Btu/kWh = 9,890,800 kWh(thermal).
FY2025 workbook divided by the chillers' nameplate COP 6.1 → 1,621,443 kWh-equivalent electricity.
(Audit finding IA-2026-04/4 later said the measured plant COP should have been used.)
