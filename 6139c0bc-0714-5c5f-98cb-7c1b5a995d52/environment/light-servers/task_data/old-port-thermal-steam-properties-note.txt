OLD PORT THERMAL LLC — STEAM PROPERTIES AND ENERGY CONTENT (customer information sheet OPT-CIS-07, rev. 2025-12)

Delivered steam: 125 psig saturated at the customer's meter flange.
  Total enthalpy, delivered steam ............................ 1,190.0 Btu/lb
  Enthalpy of condensate at 181 F (return customers) ......... 149.0 Btu/lb
  Net enthalpy for customers on the condensate return line ... 1,041.0 Btu/lb

Condensate return: available only on the Merrimack Street loop. Customers on the open-discharge service (Lowell
Industrial Park, accounts OPT-LW-09xx) discharge condensate to drain; no return credit applies and the energy
delivered is the total enthalpy of the steam.

Emission factor: Old Port Thermal reports a preliminary 2025 factor of 44.10 kg CO2e/MMBtu, calculated internally
after the biomass co-firing trial. The figure has not been verified by a third party; verification is planned for
Q1 2027 together with the 2026 figure. Customers should treat it as indicative.
