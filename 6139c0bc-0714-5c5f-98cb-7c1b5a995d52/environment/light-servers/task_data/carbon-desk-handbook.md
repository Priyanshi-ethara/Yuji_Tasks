# Carbon accounting desk — working handbook (Group Sustainability Reporting)

Last reviewed 2026-07-20 by Maren Ostrowski.

## Where the records of reference live
| Record | System | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Scope 2 protocol in force, its schedules, EFS errata, prior inventories | Confluence, space **SUST** (Group Sustainability) | The wiki copy is the controlled copy. Word copies in team folders are uncontrolled. |
| Inventory board (one item per site) | monday.com workspace "Group Sustainability" | Columns are fixed by the protocol §8. |
| Supplier electricity invoices + reissues | Box, folder "Facilities — Utility invoices FY2026" (Facilities owns it, we read) | CSV exports in our data folder are working copies only. |
| Meter register readings | Energy platform (one main meter per site) | Month-end register reads; consumption = difference of reads. |
| Certificate registry status | Airtable base "Registry account mirror" (synced nightly from NRR, AIB and Ofgem by Greenledger) | Read-only for us; retirements are instructed to the broker, never keyed in the mirror. |
| PPA and supply contracts | Salesforce (procurement keeps each signed contract as a Closed Won opportunity; terms in the Description) | |
| Supplier statements, verified factors | supplier mails to the desk mailbox + data folder | |

## Conventions
- Never retire, transfer or reallocate certificates yourself; that is the broker's job on the Head of Sustainability
  Reporting's written instruction.
- Facilities owns its own wiki space (FAC); we do not edit their pages, they do not edit ours.
- Site figures go on the board to one decimal place of t CO2e; the summary page totals are the sum of the board figures.
- Where two records disagree, the protocol says which one governs. Write down which one you used.
- Late changes happen: suppliers reissue invoices and registries correct retirements after we start. The inventory is
  struck when the summary page is published; look again before you publish.
