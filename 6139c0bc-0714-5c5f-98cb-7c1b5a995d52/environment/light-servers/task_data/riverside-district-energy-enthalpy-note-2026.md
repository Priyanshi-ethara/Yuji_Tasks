# Riverside District Energy — steam enthalpy note for customers (calendar 2026)

Issued 2026-01-08 by RDE Engineering (Hartwell, Ohio). Applies to all metered steam deliveries in 2026 and to
statements for fiscal years of customers that end in 2026.

## Delivered steam
| Property | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery pressure | 150 psig | saturated, at the customer meter |
| Total enthalpy of delivered steam (h_g) | 1,195.5 Btu/lb | from IAPWS-IF97 at 164.7 psia |
| Condensate return temperature (contract) | 180 °F | closed-loop customers only |
| Enthalpy of returned condensate (h_f at 180 °F) | 148.0 Btu/lb | credited only to closed-loop customers |
| **Net delivered enthalpy, closed-loop customers** | **1,047.5 Btu/lb** | h_g − h_f; use for energy accounting where condensate is returned |

## Which customers are closed-loop
Closed-loop customers return condensate to the RDE network and receive the condensate credit on every statement
line ("CR" flag on the meter). Account RDE-HW-2207 (Talvik Precision Components, Hartwell) has been closed-loop
since the 2019 return-line project; its monthly statements carry the CR flag.

## Emission factor
RDE's 2025 plant emission factor, verified by Arden Assurance LLP (limited assurance, ISAE 3410) on 2026-03-20,
is 58.20 kg CO2e per MMBtu of delivered steam. The verification statement is available on request and was sent to
customer sustainability contacts with the FY statements.
