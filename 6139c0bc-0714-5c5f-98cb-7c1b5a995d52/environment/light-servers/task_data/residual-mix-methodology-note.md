# Residual mix — why the market-based figure is not zero without instruments

The residual mix is the emission intensity of the electricity left over after all tracked renewable attributes have
been claimed. A site without a qualifying instrument reports its purchased electricity at the residual-mix factor of its
region, which is usually higher than the location (grid-average) factor. Where a factor set publishes no residual mix for
a region, the protocol in force says what to use instead. Instruments reduce only the MWh they cover; the remainder of
the site's electricity stays at the residual mix. Chilled water bought from a plant is not covered by the site's
certificates, because the certificates were retired against the site's own electricity purchases.
