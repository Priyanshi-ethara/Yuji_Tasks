# FAQ — Emission factor sets (written for the Rev 4 consultation, May 2026)

**Q. When EFS-2026 is published in August, do we switch to it for FY2026?**
A. The consultation draft proposed using the newest edition published before the inventory is struck (as Rev 3
does). The final Rev 4 text and its Schedule A decide; check the controlled copy on the wiki.

**Q. Do errata change the CSV?**
A. No. Errata raise the revision number and are published on the wiki; the CSV stays as first distributed.

**Q. What if a region has no residual-mix factor?**
A. Rev 3 said use the national or interconnection average. Rev 4 changes this — see §7.

**Q. Are steam factors in t CO2e/MWh?**
A. No: district steam defaults are kg CO2e per MMBtu of delivered steam energy.
