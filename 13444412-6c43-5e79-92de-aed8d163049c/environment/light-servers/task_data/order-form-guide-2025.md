# Order forms - what the clauses mean (Sales Ops, 2025)

Every account that signs with Sales has an order form in DocuSign. The desk only needs four clauses:

1. **Plan and seats** - the starting plan and seat count.
2. **Term** - for annual plans, the 12-month term. For monthly plans, usually none.
3. **Committed term** - an optional minimum period (12, 24 or 36 months) for which the account has agreed to keep a
   subscription. Within a committed term the account cannot cancel; it may change plan or seats within the limits of
   clause 4. The committed term is shown in the order form's title and in clause 3.
4. **Minimum seats** - where present, the lowest seat count allowed during the committed term.

Self-serve accounts (signed up in the app) have no order form and no committed term.
