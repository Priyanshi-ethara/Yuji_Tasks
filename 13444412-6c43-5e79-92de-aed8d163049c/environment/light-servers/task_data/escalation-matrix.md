# Escalation matrix

| Situation | Who decides | Desk action |
|---|---|---|
| Refund, credit, chargeback | Finance (Lou Varga) | refund sentence in the reply; nothing in Stripe/QuickBooks |
| Discount or free month | Customer Success + Finance | none; never promise |
| Contract or committed-term question | Sales Ops | read the order form; never edit it |
| Price card looks wrong | Revenue Operations (Anouk Brennan-Price) | ask in #revops; use the price book as it stands |
| Account manager asks to hold a customer instruction | Billing Desk lead | process under BD-STD-2; AM may contact the customer |
| Sender may not be the billing contact | Billing Desk | hold under BD-STD-2 section 3 |
