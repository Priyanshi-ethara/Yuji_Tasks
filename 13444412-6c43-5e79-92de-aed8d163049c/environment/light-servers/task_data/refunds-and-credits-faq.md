# Refunds and credits - FAQ for the billing desk (Finance, October 2026)

**Can the desk refund a charge in Stripe?** No. Since the October audit (FIN-2210) every refund, partial refund,
credit note or goodwill gesture is raised and decided by Finance. The desk adds the refund sentence from the reply
templates and leaves the decision to Finance.

**What if Customer Success or an account manager asks the desk to refund?** Point them to Finance. A request from a
colleague does not change who may issue refunds.

**Does a downgrade create a credit?** No. Downgrades take effect at the end of the paid period, so nothing has been
over-charged.

**Can we offer a free month instead?** No - retention offers belong to Customer Success and need Finance approval
and a signed order form.

**QuickBooks?** QuickBooks is Finance's ledger. The desk can look, never post.
