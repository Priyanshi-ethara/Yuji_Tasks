# Annual billing - customer FAQ (Customer Success, June 2026)

**How much do I save on annual?** On Grid, annual billing works out at £20.00 per seat per month against £24.00
monthly. On Mosaic, £29.00 per seat per month against £35.00. Line is £10.00 a month on annual against £12.00.
(Figures from the March 2025 price list.)

**When does a switch to annual start?** At your next renewal - the end of the month you have paid for.

**Can I switch back?** Yes, at the end of the annual term.

**Do seats change?** No; seats carry over. Add seats any time; extra seats are pro-rated to the annual term end.
