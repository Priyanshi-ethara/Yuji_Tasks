# Retention playbook 2026 (Customer Success)

Tools CS may propose, never the desk: (1) up to 10% off an annual renewal; (2) one free month on Line; (3) training
credits. Each needs Finance approval and a signed order form before it exists. Proposed offers are logged in the
Retention offers table; a "Proposed" row is not an offer the customer has.

When a customer asks to cancel, CS may ask the desk to wait. The desk processes the customer's instruction under
BD-STD-2 regardless; CS can reach out to the customer in parallel.
