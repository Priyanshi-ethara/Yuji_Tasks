# Billing team handbook — business half-hourly accounts (extract)

Owner: Imogen Ashdale · last reviewed 2 Sep 2026

## 1. What a billing run is
A run (BR-yymm) bills every half-hourly business account on the BHH tariff for one period of local civil days,
normally the 15th of one month to the 14th of the next. The analyst drafts one invoice per site in the ledger;
the Billing Manager approves the drafts; Credit Control issues them and collects by direct debit. Analysts never
issue, authorise or collect.

## 2. Where things are
- Method and sources of record: BP-4 in the Billing & Tariffs space. Where this handbook and BP-4 differ, BP-4
  governs.
- Tariff editions: one page per edition in the same space.
- Site accounts: the CRM (supply dates, ledger customer, billing contact).
- Meters: the metering operations base (meter profiles, collector deliveries) and the metering asset register.
- Interval data: the collector exports for the run, delivered to the run folder.
- Ledger: QuickBooks. Xero holds history before 2026 and is read-only.

## 3. Habits that have saved us
- Read the account before you bill it: supply dates move.
- Never key a figure from last month's run into this month's; the rates and the calendar change.
- Keep the line names exactly as BP-4 gives them; Credit Control's posting rules key on them.
- When a number looks odd, find the record that explains it; do not smooth it.
- Do not contact customers from the billing mailbox during a run. Queries go to the account manager.

## 4. Approvals
The Billing Manager wants one summary mail per run once every draft exists, with the subject line BP-4 gives.
