# The capacity charge — how it is worked out (BHH)

The capacity charge pays for the network capacity your site uses at its busiest. It is set from your maximum
demand: the highest average power, in kW, that your site drew over any single half-hour.

- Demand in a half-hour (kW) is the energy used in that half-hour (kWh) divided by half an hour, i.e. kWh × 2.
- The maximum demand is taken separately within the days each tariff edition covers in the bill, and that edition's
  rate (pence per kW per day) is applied to it for the number of those days that the site was on supply.
- Maximum demand is stated in kW to two decimal places.

Example: a highest half-hour of 61.5 kWh is a maximum demand of 123.00 kW. At 4.800 p/kW/day over 17 days the
charge is 123.00 × 17 × £0.048 = £100.37.
