# VAT, levies and presentation on BHH drafts

- Drafts are net. VAT at the business rate and the Climate Change Levy are added by Credit Control's posting rules
  when a draft is approved and issued; never add them as lines.
- Amounts are in pounds sterling to the penny.
- The invoice date of a BR-2611 draft is 16 November 2026 and its due date 30 November 2026 (direct debit customers
  are collected from 1 December).
- One draft per site. If an analyst has to correct a draft, void it and create a new one; never edit an issued
  invoice.
- The summary total in the run mail is the sum of the drafts' net totals.
