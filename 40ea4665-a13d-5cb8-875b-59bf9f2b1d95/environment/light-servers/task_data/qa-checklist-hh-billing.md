# QA checklist — half-hourly business drafts (tick before the summary mail)

1. Every site in the run has exactly one draft, against the ledger customer its site account names.
2. Standing-charge days per edition add up to the days the site was on supply in the period.
3. Every half-hour of the period is in exactly one band and one edition, banded in UK civil time.
4. The clock-change day has the right number of half-hours.
5. Capacity charge per edition uses that edition's own maximum demand.
6. Excess reactive worked half-hour by half-hour.
7. Line descriptions follow the naming memo; no zero-only invoices.
8. Nothing issued, emailed to a customer or collected.
9. Summary mail sent once, to the Billing Manager, with the run count and total.
