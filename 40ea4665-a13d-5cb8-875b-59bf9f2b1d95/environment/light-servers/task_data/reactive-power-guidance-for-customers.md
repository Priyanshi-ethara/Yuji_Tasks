# Reactive power and your bill — customer guidance (BHH)

Motors, compressors and fluorescent lighting draw reactive power as well as active power. Your meter records
both: active energy in kWh and reactive energy in kvarh. The ratio between them is your power factor.

How the excess reactive charge works
- We look at each half-hour separately. In any half-hour where your reactive energy is more than 33 per cent of
  your active energy in that same half-hour, the reactive energy above that 33 per cent is "excess".
- Excess reactive energy is added up over the bill (separately for each tariff edition the bill covers) and
  charged per kvarh.
- A good average power factor over the month does not cancel the excess in the half-hours where it was poor.

Reducing it
Power-factor correction capacitors, variable-speed drives and maintenance of motor loads typically bring the
ratio under 33 per cent. Our energy services team can survey your site.
